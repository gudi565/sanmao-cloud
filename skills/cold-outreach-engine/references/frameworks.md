# Message Frameworks

Six field-tested structures. Choose based on the **type of signal** you have, not on personal preference.

## 1. Observation → Hypothesis → Question  *(default)*
Best when you have a Tier-3 signal (something they said or did).

> Saw your post about onboarding drop-off. Most teams with your funnel shape lose 40%+ at step 3 because the activation email fires within the first hour. Is step 3 where you're bleeding right now?

**Shape:** [Specific observation, quoted] → [Your hypothesis tying it to an outcome] → [Yes/no question]

## 2. Peer Case Study
Best when you have proof from a similar company.

> We just helped [peer company, same stage/vertical] cut onboarding drop-off from 40% to 12% in 6 weeks by delaying the activation email. Worth me sending the one-page writeup of how?

**Shape:** [Peer outcome with numbers] → [Asset offer]

## 3. Resource Give
Best when you have **no direct angle yet** — give value first, ask nothing. Use when research found no Tier-3 signal.

> Read your post on onboarding. I tore down 3 fintech onboarding flows last month — [link]. No pitch, figured it'd be useful given what you wrote.

**Shape:** [Reference what they said] → [Give a relevant asset] → [Explicit "no pitch"]

## 4. Trigger Event
Best tied to funding, hiring, or launch — Tier-2 signals.

> Saw you raised Series B last week and posted two PM roles. Onboarding usually breaks right after a B round when growth outpaces the funnel. Open to comparing notes?

**Shape:** [Named trigger] → [Why it matters now] → [Low-friction CTA]

## 5. Problem Hypothesis  *(high risk, high reward)*
A bold, specific guess at their problem. Use only when your research strongly supports it.

> Quick guess based on your funnel shape: your activation email fires within hour 1 and you're losing 35%+ there. Close?

**Shape:** [Specific guess] → ["Close?" / "Am I off?"] — invites correction, which is a reply.

## 6. The Breakup  *(Touch 3 only)*
Give them permission to say no. Paradoxically raises reply rate.

> No reply — totally fair, timing's everything. Closing the loop so I'm not cluttering your inbox: should I cross you off, or circle back in Q4?

**Shape:** [Acknowledge silence without guilt] → [Binary CTA: off / later]

## CTA hierarchy (low → high friction)
Pick the **lowest-friction CTA the situation allows.** Cold outreach should sit in the bottom half of this list.

| Friction | CTA type | Example | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lowest | **Asset** | "want the teardown?" | Cold, any signal |
| Low | **Yes/no** | "is step 3 your biggest leak?" | Cold default |
| Medium | **Interest** | "worth a chat?" | Warm-ish |
| High | **Time** | "free Thursday 2pm?" | Save for warm/inbound |

**One CTA per touch. Always.** Multiple CTAs produce zero action.

## Subject lines (email)
- ≤5 words. Often lowercase outperforms Title Case.
- Best subject is a fragment of the signal itself: *"onboarding at step 3"* / *"your post on activation"*
- ❌ Never "Quick question," "Following up," "Introduction," or anything with "!" or ALL CAPS.
- ❌ Never the prospect's name alone as the subject — it screams automation.
